# Design guide

Read [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) before making design decisions. It is the sole source of general design principles; do not invent a competing style checklist.

Apply it within the requested scope and the product's requirements, shipped behavior, and existing design system. A product's own `DESIGN.md` records product-specific decisions and is separate from this guide.

Handle focused improvements directly. For new interfaces or explicit redesigns, resolve the design intent and structural choices that materially affect the result before detailed styling. Use the project's existing tools and components; no additional CLI, package, framework, or mandatory research process is required.

Review the rendered result and relevant states when available and authorized. Report the changes, important design reasoning, and any verification limits briefly. This skill does not authorize GUI control, external publishing, or paid services.
